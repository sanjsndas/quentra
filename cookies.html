Cookies Policy

How we use cookies to make your experience as golden and fluffy as our pancakes.

📅 last updated: april 2026

Our cookie philosophy

At Quentra · Pancake Ripple, we use cookies to enhance functionality, analyze traffic, and remember your preferences — just like the perfect flip, we keep it light and precise.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us recognize you, remember your settings, and understand how you interact with our content. We use both session and persistent cookies.

2. Types of cookies we use

We categorize cookies by their purpose. Below is a detailed breakdown:

CategoryPurposeDuration
EssentialRequired for basic site functionality (e.g., navigation, security).Session
AnalyticsCollect anonymous usage data to improve content and performance.Up to 24 months
PreferenceRemember your settings (e.g., language, dark mode).12 months
MarketingUsed only if you opt-in; helps deliver relevant content.12 months

We do not use third-party tracking cookies without your explicit consent.

3. Your control

You can manage or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or remove cookies, though this may affect some features.

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

You can also adjust your cookie preferences directly on our site via the floating cookie banner (if enabled).

4. Consent & updates

By using our site, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy. We may update this policy occasionally — any changes will be posted here with a revised date. We encourage you to review it periodically.

  • You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing cookies or adjusting your settings.
  • We treat your data with the same care we use for our signature batter.

For any questions about our cookie usage, please contact us at privacy@quentra-pancake.com.
