Privacy Policy

How we handle your information — with the same care we use for a perfect golden-brown flip.

🔒 last updated: april 2026

Our commitment to privacy

At Quentra · Pancake Ripple, we believe that your personal data deserves the same respect as a well‑rested batter. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Information we collect

We may collect the following types of information when you interact with our site:

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone number (only when you voluntarily provide them via forms).
  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent, and referral sources, collected through cookies and analytics.
  • Device & browser — IP address, operating system, and browser type for security and performance.

We never sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

2. How we use your data

Your information helps us to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support.
  • Improve our content, recipes, and user experience.
  • Send occasional updates (only if you have opted in).
  • Monitor site performance and prevent fraud.

We process your data based on legitimate interest and, where required, your explicit consent.

3. Cookies & tracking

We use minimal cookies to enhance functionality and analyze traffic. You can manage your preferences via our Cookies Policy.

  • Essential — required for basic site operation.
  • Analytics — anonymized performance data.
  • Preference — remember your settings.

You may adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies, though this may affect some features.

4. Data security & retention

We implement industry-standard measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration. Personal information is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined, or as required by law.

  • All data is encrypted in transit (SSL/TLS).
  • Access is restricted to authorized personnel only.
  • We regularly review our security practices.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, or if you have any privacy-related questions, please contact us at privacy@quentra-pancake.com.

  • Request a copy of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

This policy may be updated occasionally. We will notify you of any material changes via our website or email.
